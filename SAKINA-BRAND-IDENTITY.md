## 🎯 VISION

### "Empower Muslims with Sovereign, Intelligent, and Trustworthy Islamic Guidance"

A world where Islamic knowledge is:
- **Accessible** to everyone, everywhere
- **Reliable** without hallucinations or inaccuracies
- **Private** with complete data sovereignty
- **Respectful** of Islamic scholarship and traditions
- **Empowering** for personal spiritual growth

---

## 💡 MISSION

Project Sakina's mission is to:

1. **Provide Zero-Hallucination Islamic Guidance**
   - Strict RAG guardrails ensure only verified Islamic knowledge is shared
   - Similarity threshold of 0.85 prevents inaccurate responses
   - Every answer is traced to authenticated Islamic sources

2. **Ensure Complete Privacy & Sovereignty**
   - All processing happens locally
   - No cloud APIs = no data exfiltration
   - Full encryption at rest and in transit
   - Users control their data completely

3. **Respect Islamic Scholarship Traditions**
   - Support all four Madhahib (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafii, Hanbali)
   - Verify sources with Islamic scholars
   - Preserve authenticity grades (Sahih, Hasan, Weak)
   - Cite all sources transparently

4. **Build with Transparency & Trust**
   - Open source architecture
   - Append-only audit logs
   - Clear documentation of all processes
   - Community-driven development

5. **Create an Offline-Capable System**
   - Works without internet after initialization
   - No dependency on external services
   - Complete local autonomy
   - Reliable in any environment
